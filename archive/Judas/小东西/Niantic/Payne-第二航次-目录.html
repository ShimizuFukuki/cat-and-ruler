        George S. Payne journal of a voyage from New York towards Canton, Ship Niantic, 1836 Nov 6-1837 Oct 14
        This is second of four handwritten journals containing the observations and musings of able seaman and second mate George S. Payne while sailing on his four separate year-long round trip voyages from New York City to China.
